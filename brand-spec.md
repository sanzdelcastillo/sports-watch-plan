# Watch Plan — Matchday Editorial Design System

**Status:** Approved direction for the clickable validation prototype  
**Selected:** September 6, 2026  
**Mode:** Extension of approved visual direction A

## Design read

The artifact is a phone-first, responsive planning product for U.S.-based soccer fans who follow multiple clubs and competitions. Its visual language combines the urgency of a matchday programme with the clarity of a trustworthy calendar. Visual variance is 6/10, motion intensity 4/10, information density 7/10, asset dependence 2/10, and current brand fidelity 5/10 because the product name remains provisional.

## Positioning

The primary screen is an active planning surface rather than a marketing page. It must read at phone distance, remain useful on a laptop, and make uncertainty feel intentional. The visual temperature is energetic and assured without implying certainty the data does not support.

## Product rules expressed visually

Viewing options are visually separate from fixture identity. Verified, package-dependent, stale, replay, unknown, changed, postponed, and unavailable states each have distinct wording and treatment. Provider display never implies user entitlement. Timezone appears near every time context. Must-watch and reminder actions are independent. Spoiler protection uses copy as well as iconography. Team and broadcaster logos are excluded until display rights are confirmed.

## Working identity

The wordmark **WATCHPLAN** is a provisional typographic mark, not a final logo. Team names and competition labels are text-only scenario content. The interface must display “Prototype data — not live” during validation.
